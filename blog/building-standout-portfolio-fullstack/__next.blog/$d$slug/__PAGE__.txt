<p>Don't just say "I built a chat app." Explain why you chose WebSockets over API polling, and how you handled message persistence and security.</p>

<p>Include snippets of your architectural diagrams or wireframes. Recruiters and clients want to see how you think, not just how you code.</p>

<p>Two deep, well-documented projects are better than ten simple "todo" apps. Each case study should cover:</p>

<li>The challenge.</li>
<li>Your solution.</li>
<li>The results (e.g., "Reduced load time by 40%").</li>
</ul>
